# Feature Spec 001: TuneLedger Music Copyright & Sample Clearance Protocol

## 1. Overview
TuneLedger provides automated sample clearance and streaming royalty splits for music producers, beatmakers, and record labels. GenLayer's AI musicology oracle evaluates audio sample duration and melodic similarity to calculate and disburse on-chain royalties autonomously.

## 2. Requirements & Workflow
- Original Work Registration: Register track title, ISRC, and fingerprint hash.
- Sample Clearance Agreement: Producer requests clearance, specifies sample duration, and deposits fee.
- Musicology AI Consensus: Validates sample overlap and sets binding royalty split BPS.
- Streaming Royalty Distribution: Instant autonomous split between master owner and derivative producer.
